    Where the idea comes from

    Everyone knows the feeling. You look up one small thing, an hour later you are reading about something else entirely, and you can't quite say how you got there. People call it falling down a rabbit hole.

    RabbitHole is built to make that happen on purpose, and with history. You start somewhere on the map, at some point in the last twelve thousand years. Two facts appear. You pick the one that pulls at you, and the next two follow from it. Nobody tells you what you ought to find interesting.

    There are no accounts, no adverts and no feed designed to keep you scrolling. Just the past, and your own curiosity deciding where to go next.
